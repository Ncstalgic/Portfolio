Personal Interests & Hobbies

Outside of classes and coding assignments, here are a few things I enjoy doing in my free time:

Game Design & 3D Modeling

In my free time, I love modeling custom anime-styled weapons and props in Blender. Beyond just building the 3D assests, I also enjoy animating and scripting anime inspired combat moves and attacks, with visual effects(VFX).

Digital Illustration & Art

Outside of coding and game design, I spend a lot of time drawing my favorite anime characters and practicing anime-style digital art. I also help out my friends by designing and illustrating custom character sheets for the stories they are writing.
